# Forward-Testing Protocol and Initial Record

## Protocol

Use two materially different projects whenever the contract or validator changes:

1. A local-service brand with owner/operator identity, geography, proof, safety or credential claims, tools or educational acquisition, social or physical collateral, and possible service/resource brand architecture.
2. A software or developer-product brand with category and naming questions, repository/documentation expression, technical proof, founder/project identity, and no local-service assumptions.

Keep audited projects read-only. Use disposable output directories. Give the execution only the skill, project locator, allowed evidence, and audit request; do not seed expected findings.

Evaluate:

- conditional module activation;
- project-specific evidence and audience consequences;
- claim inventory completeness;
- owner-decision separation;
- multimodal and competitor limitations;
- retained strengths and preservation constraints;
- implementation-versus-outcome evidence;
- dependency order;
- canonical JSON/Markdown reconciliation;
- validator failure quality and nested type safety.

Run the renderer and validator against both complete handoffs. Critique missed material issues, generic findings, unsupported claims, inflated visual severity, weak acceptance criteria, hidden blockers, and future-revision ambiguity. Improve only reusable failure classes.

## Initial implementation record — Omaha Tree Care / Midwest Roots

- **Target and revision:** read-only `sudotsu/omahatreecare` checkout at `85366d894122591bea007e0265c31605697a31ec`, reconciled with `https://omahatreecare.com` on 2026-08-08.
- **Disposable output:** an OS-temporary `brand-forward-omaha` directory; four findings, four claims, five evidence sources, 12 modules, every required facet, three surface samples, two named competitors, two material limitations, and all narrative reconciliations.
- **Observed surfaces:** pinned source plus HTTP 200 live HTML and full-page Chromium captures at 1440px and 390px. The installed `agent-browser` helper was unavailable; Playwright Chromium supplied the rendered fallback. No form submission, customer interview, authenticated social/review access, sales collateral, or physical collateral was available.
- **Material results:** the audit separated the owner decision about `Midwest Roots Tree Services` versus `Omaha Tree Care` from implementation; identified legacy location copy that exceeds the current evidence and authority discipline; recorded the tool-to-operator message bridge as an opportunity rather than measured confusion; and preserved the specific, no-account, low-pressure educational system as a strength.
- **Competitive evidence:** current first-party public surfaces for Tree Services of Omaha and Brian's Tree Service established service-category and authority conventions without treating their claims as independently verified.
- **Result:** provisional by design. The renderer and validator passed before and after reusable hardening. The audited checkout and its pre-existing untracked artifacts remained unchanged.
- **Reusable defects exposed:** a declared review method could cite an incompatible evidence class; a finding could be mapped to a module other than its canonical `module`; and the known competitive-landscape narrative omission lacked an explicit regression test.
- **Hardening changes:** exact facet-specific method-evidence observations and roles; method-to-evidence-class validation; access-to-facet consistency; exact one-module finding mapping; substantive README and narrative section gates; repeated-paragraph, facet-label-boilerplate, and policy-only conclusive-result rejection; semantic action-to-finding reconciliation; evidence-path containment, existence, minimum substance, and traversal rejection; production-revision alignment evidence; surface-sample method checks; competitor-dependent finding evidence; and a dedicated `08-competitive-brand-landscape.md` reconciliation test. These raised the regression suite from 20 to 36 tests.
- **Independent-gate correction:** the first structurally valid handoff was rejected because its narratives were thin, channel facets contradicted access state, evidence manifests omitted the actual captures, production alignment was overclaimed, and some comparative consequences lacked competitor links. The rebuilt handoff bundles the live HTML and both screenshots, uses required substantive narrative sections, marks production alignment unverified, reconciles actionable competitive prose to `STR-001`, and records unavailable review/email/physical surfaces as blocked or partial rather than passed.
- **Second independent-gate correction:** the next valid handoff was rejected because module-level prose was repeated across facets, narrative paragraphs repeated, one comparative population claim exceeded its evidence, and source/competitor manifests lacked excerpts or captures. The final handoff has 80 distinct facet-specific results, rejects trivial facet-label variation and duplicate substantive paragraphs, removes the unsupported population comparison, embeds exact bounded source excerpts, and bundles dated HTML for every competitor sample.

## Initial implementation record — opend-ai

- **Target and revision:** read-only `sudotsu/opend-ai` checkout at `69291c115435f33cf121ba44f7437d2cce8cfd46`, reconciled with `https://github.com/sudotsu/opend-ai` on 2026-08-08.
- **Disposable output:** an OS-temporary `brand-forward-opend` directory; four findings, four claims, five evidence sources, 12 modules, every required facet, three surface samples, two named competitors, two material limitations, and all narrative reconciliations.
- **Observed surfaces:** pinned source and public GitHub README with full-page Chromium captures at 1440px and 390px. No live provider, positive Bubblewrap, npm-registry, audience, or activation test was performed, so source wiring and deterministic tests were not promoted to operational proof.
- **Material results:** the audit reconciled the package metadata's broad provider-agnostic claim against the README's explicit experimental and unverified states; defined the implicit `opend-ai` repository/package versus `opend` product/command naming issue without prescribing a rename; identified the long path to the current trustworthy install route; and preserved founder candor, real failure evidence, and execution-boundary precision.
- **Competitive evidence:** current first-party Aider and anomalyco/OpenCode surfaces established category, installation, distribution, and proof-placement conventions while recording their much broader scope.
- **Result:** provisional by design. The renderer and validator passed before and after reusable hardening. The audited checkout and its pre-existing untracked teardown directory remained unchanged.
- **Reusable defects exposed:** the same method/evidence and module-mapping weaknesses reproduced while the broader claim schema correctly handled evidence that both supports and contradicts a public claim.
- **Hardening changes:** the shared validator and negative tests described in the Omaha record; the hardened validator then rejected the forward output until its method-specific evidence links, access/facet states, narratives, artifact package, production status, and competitor-dependent finding links were corrected, demonstrating that the new gate operates on full handoffs rather than fixtures alone.
- **Independent-gate correction:** the rebuilt handoff bundles both GitHub captures, records the audited feature revision as unaligned with the public default branch, treats social/review/sales/email/physical facets as not applicable instead of observed, and expands every narrative through the exact report-contract sections with canonical claim, sample, competitor, and limitation evidence.

An independent review should be repeated whenever the contract changes materially. This initial run used a separate reviewer after the two handoffs validated; the main execution remained responsible for reproducing any reported defect against current files before changing the package.

Do not describe schema fixtures as full forward tests. Full tests require complete project-specific handoffs with facet coverage, multiple warranted findings or strengths, claim inventories, surface samples, competitor state or evidenced unavailability, and critical review after validation.
