# Brand Audit Methodology

Use this as a conditional investigation map. Every module ends as passed, failed, partial, blocked, not tested, or not applicable in `coverage.json`.

## Brand thesis and audience model

Express the brand thesis as: for a specific audience in a specific situation, the project offers a specific outcome through a particular product, service, or point of view, with a supportable reason to choose it.

Test stakeholder intent against the product, pricing or offer structure, page hierarchy, proof, calls to action, customer journey, and channel expression. Distinguish buyers, users, influencers, blockers, and audiences affected by financial, safety, reputational, or adoption risk.

## Required modules and facets

### 1. Business, market, and audience

- `business_offer` — what is sold and received.
- `audience_roles` — primary and secondary audiences plus buyer/user/influencer/blocker roles.
- `triggers_outcomes` — triggering problems and desired outcomes.
- `risks_objections` — material perceived risks and objections.
- `proof_needs` — proof each audience needs before acting.

### 2. Positioning and differentiation

- `category_clarity`
- `target_customer_clarity`
- `problem_outcome_clarity`
- `choice_reason`
- `supportable_differentiators`
- `experience_alignment`

Reject interchangeable category adjectives such as trusted, innovative, professional, quality, or customer-first unless evidence and project specificity make them meaningful.

### 3. Brand architecture

- `brand_inventory`
- `ownership_relationships`
- `naming_domains_socials`
- `endorsement_and_hierarchy`
- `collision_or_dilution`

Activate this module for every project. A single-brand project still needs one bounded check proving that no competing identity was found.

### 4. Message hierarchy and comprehension

- `five_second_comprehension`
- `thirty_second_comprehension`
- `deep_comprehension`
- `promise_support_proof_cta`
- `metadata_heading_visual_alignment`
- `mobile_comprehension`

Record the tested page or artifact, viewport, state, and observation. Do not infer actual audience comprehension from reviewer intuition; label it controlled reviewer observation unless audience evidence exists.

### 5. Offer clarity and customer journey

- `offer_inventory`
- `deliverable_scope_process`
- `cta_next_step`
- `readiness_and_entry_points`
- `price_guarantee_availability`
- `offer_dilution`

Test calls to action safely. Do not submit deceptive leads, purchases, or outreach.

### 6. Trust, proof, and authority

- `reviews_testimonials`
- `case_work_outcomes`
- `credentials_licensing_certifications`
- `team_owner_local_presence`
- `process_safety_transparency`
- `technical_or_repository_proof`
- `proof_placement_and_recency`

Reconcile this module with the canonical claim inventory. Existing proof can still be a finding when it is buried, stale, weak, or unrelated to the decision it is meant to support.

### 7. Voice and verbal identity

- `recognizable_voice`
- `audience_and_situation_fit`
- `cross_channel_consistency`
- `confidence_humility`
- `generic_or_ai_phrasing`
- `manipulation_and_urgency`
- `authentic_voice_preservation`

Consistency does not require identical wording. Protect useful founder, operator, technical, local, or community voice from generic professionalization.

### 8. Visual identity and recognition

- `logo_wordmark`
- `color_typography`
- `layout_hierarchy_legibility`
- `imagery_iconography_video`
- `mobile_and_format_behavior`
- `category_distinctiveness`
- `offer_tone_alignment`
- `asset_provenance`

Evaluate only available evidence. Use audience, comprehension, trust, recognition, accessibility, or conversion consequences rather than taste. Route contrast and accessibility compliance to the responsible discipline while recording their brand consequence.

### 9. Channel expression

- `website_and_landing_pages`
- `social_and_video`
- `email_sales_documents`
- `profiles_listings_repositories`
- `physical_collateral`
- `channel_specific_adaptation`
- `identity_contact_consistency`

Activate only relevant surfaces. A not-applicable channel needs a project-specific reason.

### 10. Competitive brand landscape

- `competitor_selection`
- `category_language`
- `visual_conventions`
- `trust_conventions`
- `offer_conventions`
- `competitor_strengths`
- `crowded_and_open_positions`
- `strategic_consequence`

Capture named competitors or alternatives, dated public evidence, and the strategic consequence. When reliable evidence is inaccessible, mark the module provisional rather than inventing a landscape.

Treat the comparison set as individual evidence, not a count. For every observed named competitor, retain evidence that identifies the exact entity or locator and record at least one source-specific observation. Shared category conventions belong in the synthesis; do not copy the same full profile into multiple canonical competitor records. Distinguish the strongest comparator for each relevant dimension from the merely typical examples so “top competitor” does not become a generic composite that no actual competitor demonstrates.

### 11. Brand risk and claim discipline

- `credential_and_authority_risk`
- `superlatives_and_overpromise`
- `urgency_guarantee_pricing`
- `availability_service_area_staffing`
- `identity_contact_conflicts`
- `derivative_language_or_assets`
- `product_experience_contradictions`
- `legal_review_boundary`

Identify potential review needs without giving legal conclusions. Record the responsible downstream discipline.

### 12. Strategic preservation

- `authentic_voice`
- `recognizable_assets`
- `specificity_and_usefulness`
- `verified_proof`
- `low_pressure_paths`
- `honest_limitations`
- `channel_variation`

At least one retained strength is normally expected. If direct evidence genuinely supports none, state why in `audit.zero_strengths_justification`.

## Project-type activation

- Local service: emphasize geography, owner/operator presence, safety, credentials, reviews, estimates, vehicles, uniforms, flyers, and service/resource brand relationships.
- Software or developer product: emphasize category and command/product naming, README/docs/repository expression, technical proof, installation promise, open-source or product credibility, and technical-audience voice.
- SaaS: emphasize audience roles, trial/demo paths, plan and pricing claims, proof, security or integration claims, onboarding, and lifecycle messaging.
- Ecommerce: emphasize product architecture, merchandising promises, reviews, pricing, delivery/returns, packaging, photography provenance, and post-purchase expression.
- Creator/media: emphasize person/publication relationships, recurring formats, sponsorship or offer clarity, platform dependency, voice, and community proof.
- Nonprofit: emphasize mission/audience clarity, program and donation paths, outcome proof, governance, beneficiary dignity, and claim discipline.
- Multi-brand: emphasize parent/sub-brand endorsement, portfolio logic, domain/social ownership, cross-selling, cannibalization, and identity collisions.

## Comprehension observations

Use the same representative surface for the three time horizons when possible:

1. Five seconds: category, audience cue, and primary promise.
2. Thirty seconds: offer, meaningful differentiator, proof, and next step.
3. Several minutes: process, objections, limitations, architecture, and confidence to act.

State whether the result is reviewer observation, moderated audience evidence, behavioral data, or an inference. Do not present reviewer comprehension as measured customer perception.

## Completion discipline

An applicable module is not complete until each required facet has a surface check. Track exact methods and evidence while working. Missing customer research, analytics, social access, or physical collateral narrows conclusions and may force a provisional verdict; it never excuses unfinished source, live-site, public-profile, or competitor work that remains available.
